```text
ARRI Alexa look — filmic latitude, gentle organic softness NOT clinical digital sharpness,
fine 35mm grain, Black Pro-Mist halation bloom, anamorphic oval bokeh, crushed blacks,
controlled low saturation, true chiaroscuro contrast, never flat video-look.
Shallow depth of field, razor-thin focus plane, clear FG/MG/BG three-layer separation.
LOW-KEY by default: deep controlled shadows, single motivated key, black negative fill.
NO flat front-facing eye-level composition. Every shot takes one deliberate off-axis angle.
```
