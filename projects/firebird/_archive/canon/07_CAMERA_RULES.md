# CAMERA & LENS RULES

## Lens Map
24mm = kingdoms, armies, landscapes, creatures, architecture
35mm = travel, action, pursuit, village danger
50mm = dialogue, moral confrontation, two-shots
85mm = emotional portrait, romance, restrained tension
100mm macro = marks, hands, sword detail, eyes, embers

## Camera Movement
Human drama:
- slow push-in
- slow lateral tracking
- restrained handheld
- locked-off

Action:
- controlled handheld
- low tracking
- horse-level tracking

Myth:
- stable human-scale camera
- let the creature provide the movement

## Romance Rule
- calmer camera
- fewer cuts
- 50mm / 85mm
- proximity over flashy movement

## Camera Height
Elara: mostly eye-level
Riven early: slightly low-angle
Riven later: move toward eye-level
Aureon: extreme low-angle

## Match-Cut Logic
- mountain ridge → volcanic ridge
- bridge line → sea wall
- horizon → horizon
- river curve → Firebird wing curve
- falling burning timber → floating ember
