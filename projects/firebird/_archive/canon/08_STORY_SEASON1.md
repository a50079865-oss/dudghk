# SEASON 1 — 8 EPISODE STORY MASTER

## EP1 — THE DAY THE FIREBIRD RETURNED
Aurevia border war.
Elara rescues a child from fire.
Aureon returns and marks her.
Riven is ordered to kill her.
He finds her treating both an Aurevian child and a wounded Skeldrian soldier.
He lowers his sword.

Relationship: enemy / moral curiosity

## EP2 — THE HUNTED WOMAN
Multiple factions pursue them.
Riven chooses to save civilians despite the cost.

Relationship: distrust → first understanding

## EP3 — THE WINTER WOLF
Skeldria.
Elara discovers Riven is a prince.
Aeskar appears.
Riven's Arcadian bloodline is revealed.

Relationship: trust begins

## EP4 — THE DRAGON MOUNTAINS
Draevor.
Maevra enters.
Crown fragment search.
Varkaen awakens.

Relationship: intimacy grows / near-kiss

## EP5 — WHAT THE SEA REMEMBERS
Lysara.
Thessara warns of a burning future.
Arcadia's true history emerges.
Riven appears to betray Elara.

Relationship: trust fracture

## EP6 — THE CROWN OF ASH
Seraphel argues for Elara's voluntary sacrifice.
Riven breaks from his father's path.
He returns to Elara as an equal.
First kiss.
Shared sacrifice becomes possible.

Relationship: reunion / love

## EP7 — THE SECOND END
Four kingdoms gather.
Riven's ancestral memory reveals concentrated power caused the first collapse.
The ancient beings awaken.

Relationship: love becomes moral partnership

## EP8 — THE KINGDOM OF THE FIREBIRD
Final war.
Three options:
1. sacrifice Elara
2. collapse
3. surrender monopolized sacred power and share the price

Riven chooses the third path.

Final theme:
> If a price must be paid, do not force it onto the weakest single life. Share it.
