# SUPPORTING CHARACTERS — MASTER

## HALVARD SKAEL
Age 58 — King of Skeldria — Riven's father.
Cold but highly competent ruler.
Believes kings have no right to private love.
Elara may be innocent, but he can still order her death if it protects the realm.

## EDRIC SKAEL
Age 32 — Crown Prince of Skeldria.
Politically skilled, less battlefield experience than Riven.
Never reduce him to a jealous brother-villain.

## QUEEN ISOLDE
Age 54 — Queen of Skeldria.
Riven's mother.
Her bloodline carries ancient Arcadian ancestry.
Quiet keeper of dangerous records.

## TORVAN VAERON
Age 61 — King of Draevor.
Former battlefield king.
Believes peace is preparation for the next war.

## MAEVRA VAERON
Age 29 — Crown Princess of Draevor.
Strategic, severe, politically lucid.
Old political ally / former plausible marriage candidate for Riven.
Never a jealous romantic rival.

## THESSARA NERIAN
Age 49 — Queen of Lysara.
Has read the sea's memory for years and lost much of her own memory.

## SELENE NERIAN
Age 25 — Princess of Lysara.
Distrusts magic after watching her mother lose memories.
Later becomes an ally.

## ORIAN SOLAREN
Age 56 — King of Aurevia.
His dynasty claims sacred Firebird legitimacy.
Elara threatens the foundation of his rule.

## LUCIEN SOLAREN
Age 28 — Crown Prince of Aurevia.
Intelligent, charming, protective in a way that can become control.
Wants to absorb Elara into the royal system through marriage.

## HIGH ARCHON SERAPHEL
Age 67 — Head of the Order of the Ashen Crown.
Primary ideological antagonist.

Past:
At 19, he sealed off a plague village, including his own family, to save tens of thousands.
413 people died.

Philosophy:
> “선택하지 않는 것도 선택이다.”

Flaw:
He always concentrates the price on one person or one group.

## CAEDMON VAREK
Age 41 — Leader of the Ashen Host.
Former Aurevian commander.
Rejects the current political order after participating in brutal suppression.

Philosophy:
> “썩은 나무를 치료하지 않는다. 베어내는 것이다.”
