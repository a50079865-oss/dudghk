# VISUAL BIBLE

## Core Definition
> Beautiful but brutal medieval reality interrupted by rare moments of overwhelming ancient myth.

## Film Texture

- subtle anamorphic feel
- black pro-mist
- soft halation
- restrained grain
- realistic skin texture

- rough linen
- aged leather
- blackened steel
- pale weathered stone
- dark timber
- mud
- soot
- rain stains
- frost
- ash

## Lighting

- overcast daylight
- cold winter sun
- soft dawn
- diffused cloud light

Interior:
- candles
- fireplaces
- oil lamps
- narrow windows
- real darkness allowed

## Magic Visualization
Magic should appear as reality reacting.

Asren:
- heat distortion
- ash rising upward
- stilling fire
- charcoal / crimson / amber / selected white heat

Aeskar:
- snow silence
- frost
- ice haze
- no body glow

Varkaen:
- metal vibration
- stone cracking
- pressure change
- geological scale

Nerysia:
- water becomes unnaturally still
- displaced currents
- submerged silhouette
